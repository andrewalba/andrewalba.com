Field of Dreams Whiskey is the brand, founded by former Major League Baseball pitcher Drew Storen and Andy Keller.  The company is owned by a collective of current and former MLB players.

The whiskey is distilled primarily at Cedar Ridge Distillery in Swisher, Iowa, with some blends also sourced from distilleries in Minnesota and Michigan. Notably, the brand uses authentic corn harvested from the famous Field of Dreams movie site in Dyersville, Iowa.
