A quiet chuckle reading this meditation.

Neither _exceptional_, nor _dim and lazy_. Simply human—**hindered, at times, by bad habits.**

Yet, I am fortunate. In the quiet presence of others—my wife, my family, leaders in work and community, I have seen better paths walked.

I offer simple Stoic principles, though I am no evangelist. Stoicism complements my faith, yet I shout neither from the rooftops.

Proclamation so often falls on deaf ears. Instead, I choose example: the quiet, consistent embodiment of wisdom, courage, justice, and temperance. Hearts and minds are not changed by words, but by how we live.
