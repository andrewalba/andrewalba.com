Today is a swift current. A pendulum swinging blindly between years, months, days, and hours.

Seasons fracture under the weight of shadow, whispered as *unlucky*, or bloom briefly in the sun of fortune. 
Yet balance is not found in the height of the wave, nor the depth of the trough, but in the steady keel that lets us endure.

### Conclusion

Life moves in endless tides. What feels like a curse softens into a blessing; what feels like a blessing turns unyielding. 
We hold no dominion over the winds that rise, yet the posture of our own hearts steers the vessel home.
