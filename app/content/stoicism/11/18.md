Years stretch wide since Marcus set ink to page. Yet no reader, in any hour, has found these words estranged from their own present.

Time turns. We are carried along. The noise of the day claims ignorance is new; history tells a quieter truth. We forget what came before, and so we mirror it.

Even now, many turn away from fact and inquiry—the same refusal, worn in modern dress, across millennia.

### Conclusion

We cannot compel another mind toward truth. We can only tend our own ground.

1. Hold fast to what is true.
2. Labor for the common good.
3. Align desire with what lies within our command.
4. Receive whatever nature brings.
