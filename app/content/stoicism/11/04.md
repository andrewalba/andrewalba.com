As the election season approaches, the world stirs with the quiet gravity of change. Yet change itself is neither heavy nor light; it simply arrives. **It is what it is.**

### Conclusion

As the authors of the :daily-stoic remind us, events remain uncolored and objective. It is only our own lens that names them good or ill.

We are shaped not by the shifting tides of circumstance, but by the quiet posture of our own hearts toward them.
