Epictetus **bound his choices to a higher will**. In that surrender lies quiet.

A plane grounds. A secret escapes the tongue. A career earned through years of devotion dissolves in a single memorandum.

Yet we still chart the course. We still gather to celebrate. We still reach for tomorrow.

### Conclusion

Adapt. Pivot. Accept. Persevere.

The world turns on its own terms. We remain in motion.
